Q: A ship is filled with 5973 tons of cargo. It stops in the Bahamas, where sailors load 8723 tons of cargo onboard. How many tons of cargo does the ship hold now?
A:
Method 1: Add the original cargo and the new cargo directly: 5973 + 8723 = 14696.
Method 2: Round 5973 to 6000, then adjust: 6000 + 8723 = 14723, and subtract 27 to return to the actual value: 14723 - 27 = 14696.
PrefMethod: Method 1

Q: Jason joined his school's band. He bought a flute for $142.46, a music tool for $8.89, and a song book for $7. How much did Jason spend at the music store?
A:
Method 1: Add the item prices in the order listed: 142.46 + 8.89 + 7 = 158.35.
Method 2: Group the smaller amounts first: 8.89 + 7 = 15.89, then add to the flute price: 142.46 + 15.89 = 158.35.
PrefMethod: Method 1

Q: Joan found 70 seashells on the beach. She gave Sam some of her seashells. She has 27 seashells. How many seashells did she give to Sam?
A:
Method 1: Subtract the number she has now from the original total: 70 - 27 = 43.
Method 2: Use guess-and-check to try values that reduce 70 to 27, such as 70 - 43 = 27.
PrefMethod: Method 1
