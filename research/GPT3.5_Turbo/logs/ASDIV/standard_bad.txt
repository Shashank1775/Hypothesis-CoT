A_model:
Let the father's age be F and the son's age be S.

Given:
1. F + S = 55
2. (F + S) + S = 93

From equation 1:
F = 55 - S

Substitute F = 55 - S into equation 2:
(55 - S) + S = 93
55 - S + S = 93
55 = 93
S = 93 - 55
S = 38

Substitute S = 38 into F = 55 - S:
F = 55 - 38
F = 17

Therefore, the father's present age is 17 years and the son's present age is 38 years. The answer is the answer is 17 and 38.
