A_model:
Hypothesis/Plan: Let the fixed charge be denoted by F and the charge per km be denoted by D. We can set up a system of equations using the given information and solve for F and D. Once we have the values of F and D, we can calculate the total charge for traveling 25 km.

For a journey of 15 km: F + 15D = 110

Subtracting the first equation from the second:
5D = 35
D = 7

Substitute D = 7 back into the first equation:
F + 10(7) = 75
F + 70 = 75
F = 5

Total charge = 5 + 25(7)
