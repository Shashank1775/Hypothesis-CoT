Q: A ship is filled with 5973 tons of cargo. It stops in the Bahamas, where sailors load 8723 tons of cargo onboard. How many tons of cargo does the ship hold now?
A:
Hypothesis: We need to compute the total cargo on the ship after it makes a stop. This requires adding the initial and newly loaded cargo. A direct addition should suffice.

Step 1: Known values and assumptions:

Initial cargo = 5973 tons

Additional cargo = 8723 tons

No cargo is removed or lost

Step 2: Two possible solution methods:

Method 1: Add the two amounts directly.

Method 2: Round the first value to 6000, compute, then adjust.

Step 3: Execute both methods:

Method 1: 5973 + 8723 = 14696

Method 2: Estimate 6000 + 8723 = 14723 → 14723 - 27 = 14696

Step 4: Method comparison:
Method 1 is more direct and avoids rounding errors. Method 2 is valid but unnecessarily complex.

Step 5: Preferred method (Method 1):
5973 + 8723 = 14696

Step 6: Double-check:
Add in reverse: 8723 + 5973 = 14696 ✅
the answer is 14696.0

Q: Jason joined his school's band. He bought a flute for $142.46, a music tool for $8.89, and a song book for $7. How much did Jason spend at the music store?
A:
Hypothesis: This is a sum of three items. The goal is to compute the total amount spent by adding all costs.

Step 1: Known values and assumptions:

Flute = $142.46

Tool = $8.89

Book = $7.00

No discounts or tax added

Step 2: Two possible solution methods:

Method 1: Add all prices as listed.

Method 2: Combine smaller values first, then add to the flute price.

Step 3: Execute both methods:

Method 1: 142.46 + 8.89 = 151.35; 151.35 + 7 = 158.35

Method 2: 8.89 + 7 = 15.89; 15.89 + 142.46 = 158.35

Step 4: Method comparison:
Both are valid, but Method 1 mirrors how prices appear in sequence, making it easier to follow.

Step 5: Preferred method (Method 1):
142.46 + 8.89 + 7 = 158.35

Step 6: Double-check:
Grouped sum confirms result: (8.89 + 7 = 15.89) + 142.46 = 158.35 ✅
the answer is 158.35

Q: Joan found 70 seashells on the beach. She gave Sam some of her seashells. She now has 27 seashells. How many seashells did she give to Sam?
A:
Hypothesis: We are given a starting and ending quantity. The number given away is the difference between them.

Step 1: Known values and assumptions:

Initial count: 70

Remaining: 27

No seashells lost or discarded

Step 2: Two possible solution methods:

Method 1: Subtract ending from starting count

Method 2: Try different values by guess-and-check

Step 3: Execute both methods:

Method 1: 70 - 27 = 43

Method 2: Try 43: 70 - 43 = 27 ✅

Step 4: Method comparison:
Method 1 is direct and efficient. Method 2 requires unnecessary iterations.

Step 5: Preferred method (Method 1):
70 - 27 = 43

Step 6: Double-check:
27 + 43 = 70 ✅
the answer is 43

Q: A marine biologist measured one fish that was 0.3 foot long and another that was 0.2 foot long. How much longer was the first fish?
A:
Hypothesis: This is a difference calculation. We subtract one measurement from the other.

Step 1: Known values and assumptions:

Fish 1: 0.3 ft

Fish 2: 0.2 ft

Both measurements in same unit

Step 2: Two possible methods:

Method 1: Subtract lengths in feet

Method 2: Convert to mm, subtract, then convert back

Step 3: Execute both methods:

Method 1: 0.3 - 0.2 = 0.1 ft

Method 2: 0.3 = 91.44 mm, 0.2 = 60.96 mm; diff = 30.48 mm = 0.1 ft

Step 4: Method comparison:
Method 1 is simpler and equally accurate for same-unit input.

Step 5: Preferred method (Method 1):
0.3 - 0.2 = 0.1

Step 6: Double-check:
Add: 0.2 + 0.1 = 0.3 ✅
the answer is 0.1

Q: Joan had 695 Pokémon cards, and 6 were torn. Sara bought 133 of Joan’s cards. How many Pokémon cards does Joan have now?
A:
Hypothesis: We must subtract two losses from a total: cards that were damaged and cards that were sold.

Step 1: Known values and assumptions:

Starting: 695 cards

Torn: 6 cards

Sold: 133 cards

Step 2: Two methods:

Method 1: Subtract torn, then sold

Method 2: Combine losses, subtract once

Step 3: Execute both methods:

Method 1: 695 - 6 = 689; 689 - 133 = 556

Method 2: 6 + 133 = 139; 695 - 139 = 556

Step 4: Method comparison:
Method 2 reduces operations and is cleaner.

Step 5: Preferred method (Method 2):
695 - (6 + 133) = 695 - 139 = 556

Step 6: Double-check:
556 + 133 + 6 = 695 ✅
the answer is 556
