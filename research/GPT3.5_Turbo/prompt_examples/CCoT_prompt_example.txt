Q: A ship is filled with 5973 tons of cargo. It stops in the Bahamas, where sailors load 8723 tons of cargo onboard. How many tons of cargo does the ship hold now?  
A:  
Step 1: State all given information and assumptions.  
- The ship starts with 5973 tons of cargo.  
- An additional 8723 tons are loaded.  
- We assume no cargo is removed or lost.  

Step 2: Propose two methods to solve the problem.  
Method 1: Direct addition of the initial and added cargo: 5973 + 8723.  
Method 2: Estimate 5973 as 6000, then add: 6000 + 8723 = 14723, and subtract the difference from the estimate: 14723 - 27.  

Step 3: Work through both methods step by step.  
Method 1: 5973 + 8723 = 14696.  
Method 2: 6000 + 8723 = 14723. The estimate is 27 higher than the actual, so subtract 27: 14723 - 27 = 14696.  

Step 4: Compare methods.  
- Method 1 is more precise and involves fewer steps.  
- Method 2 requires estimation and correction, introducing more room for error.  

Step 5: Choose the better method and solve.  
- Method 1 is better due to its simplicity and accuracy.  
- Solution: 5973 + 8723 = 14696.  

Step 6: Double-check the answer.  
- Re-adding: 5973 + 8723 = 14696. The calculation checks out.  

The answer is 14696.0

Q: Jason joined his school's band. He bought a flute for $142.46, a music tool for $8.89, and a song book for $7. How much did Jason spend at the music store?  
A:  
Step 1: State all given information and assumptions.  
- Jason bought three items: flute ($142.46), music tool ($8.89), song book ($7).  
- We assume all prices are in dollars and there are no taxes or discounts.  

Step 2: Propose two methods to solve the problem.  
Method 1: Add the prices in the order listed: 142.46 + 8.89 + 7.  
Method 2: Group smaller items first: (8.89 + 7) = 15.89, then add to the flute price: 142.46 + 15.89.  

Step 3: Work through both methods step by step.  
Method 1: 142.46 + 8.89 = 151.35. 151.35 + 7 = 158.35.  
Method 2: 8.89 + 7 = 15.89. 142.46 + 15.89 = 158.35.  

Step 4: Compare methods.  
- Both methods yield the same result.  
- Method 1 is more intuitive for adding from a receipt; Method 2 is helpful for mental math with smaller numbers.  

Step 5: Choose the better method and solve.  
- Method 1 is better for this context.  
- Solution: 142.46 + 8.89 + 7 = 158.35.  

Step 6: Double-check the answer.  
- Summing 142.46 + 8.89 = 151.35; 151.35 + 7 = 158.35.  

The answer is 158.35

Q: Joan found 70 seashells on the beach. She gave Sam some of her seashells. She has 27 seashells now. How many did she give to Sam?  
A:  
Step 1: State all given information and assumptions.  
- Joan started with 70 seashells and has 27 left after giving some to Sam.  
- Assume none were lost or broken, only given away.  

Step 2: Propose two methods to solve the problem.  
Method 1: Subtract the current number from the starting total: 70 - 27.  
Method 2: Use trial-and-error: Find what number subtracted from 70 results in 27.  

Step 3: Work through both methods step by step.  
Method 1: 70 - 27 = 43.  
Method 2: Try 70 - x = 27, so x = 70 - 27 = 43.  

Step 4: Compare methods.  
- Method 1 is more direct and efficient.  
- Method 2 is less practical unless guessing.  

Step 5: Choose the better method and solve.  
- Method 1 is better.  
- Solution: 70 - 27 = 43.  

Step 6: Double-check the answer.  
- 70 - 43 = 27, confirming the result.  

The answer is 43
