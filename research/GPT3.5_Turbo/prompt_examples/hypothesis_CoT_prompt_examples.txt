Q: A ship is filled with 5973 tons of cargo. It stops in the Bahamas, where sailors load 8723 tons of cargo onboard. How many tons of cargo does the ship hold now?
A:
Hypothesis/Plan: To find the total cargo after loading, we will add the initial cargo and the new cargo loaded in the Bahamas.
Step-by-step:

Initial cargo = 5973 tons

Additional cargo = 8723 tons

Total cargo = 5973 + 8723 = 14696
Answer: 14696.0

Q: Joan found 70 seashells on the beach. She gave Sam some of her seashells. She has 27 seashells. How many seashells did she give to Sam?
A:
Hypothesis/Plan: To find how many seashells Joan gave to Sam, we subtract the number she has now from the number she originally had.
Step-by-step:

Original = 70

Remaining = 27

Given away = 70 - 27 = 43
Answer: 43

Q: A marine biologist measured 1 fish that was 0.3 foot long and a second fish that was 0.2 foot long. How much longer was the first fish?
A:
Hypothesis/Plan: To find the difference in length, subtract the second fish's length from the first.
Step-by-step:

First fish = 0.3 ft

Second fish = 0.2 ft

Difference = 0.3 - 0.2 = 0.1

Q: Joan had 695 Pokémon cards, and 6 were torn. Sara bought 133 of Joan's Pokémon cards. How many Pokémon cards does Joan have now?
A:
Hypothesis/Plan: First subtract the torn cards, then subtract the cards that were sold to Sara.
Step-by-step:

Total cards = 695

After removing torn cards = 695 - 6 = 689

After selling = 689 - 133 = 556
