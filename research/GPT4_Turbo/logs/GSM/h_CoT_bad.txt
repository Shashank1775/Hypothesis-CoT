Q: Denny, an Instagram influencer, has 100000 followers and gets 1000 new followers every day. How many followers will he have if 20000 people unfollowed him in a year?

### Hypothesis/Plan:
To determine how many followers Denny will have after a year, we need to account for the daily increase in followers and the total number of unfollows. First, calculate the total number of new followers gained in a year. Then subtract the number of unfollows from the sum of the initial followers and the new followers.

### Step-by-step:

1. **Initial followers**: Denny starts with 100,000 followers.

2. **Daily follower increase**: Denny gains 1,000 followers per day.

3. **Total days in a year**: There are 365 days in a year.

4. **Total new followers in a year**:  
   \( 1,000 \times 365 = 365,000 \)

5. **Total followers after gaining new followers**:  
   \( 100,000 + 365,000 = 465,000 \)

6. **Unfollows in a year**: 20,000 people unfollowed Denny.

7. **Final follower count**:  
   \( 465,000 - 20,000 = 445,000 \)

### Hypothesis/Plan:
To determine how many pages Justin will have read after one week, we will:
1. Start with the 10 pages he read on the first day.
2. Calculate the number of pages he reads each day for the remaining 6 days, assuming he doubles the number of pages he read the previous day.
3. Add up the pages read each day to find the total.

   \( 320 \times 2 = 640 \) pages.
